## Flexible energy, connected

Skoon is a distributed energy flexibility platform. We connect grid operators, asset owners, and market parties through one platform, bringing together battery storage, flex contracts, and real-time orchestration.

By making flexibility accessible and tradeable, we help solve grid congestion and accelerate the energy transition.
